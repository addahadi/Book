Meditations · Book II

Whatever this is that I am, it is a little flesh and breath, and the ruling part. Throw away thy books; no longer distract thyself: it is not allowed.

But as if thou wast now dying, despise the flesh; it is blood and bones and a network, a contexture of nerves, veins, and arteries.

No longer let the ruling part be a slave, no longer be pulled by the strings of selfish desire, nor shrink from what the future holds.
