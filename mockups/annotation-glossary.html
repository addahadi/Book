Shared vocabulary

The marks you can leave on a page

Each card is one kind of annotation, drawn on a sample page so we mean the same thing when we name it. The badge says whether I'd put it in v1, push it to later, or note it's the scan fallback.

Highlight v1

The mind that is anxious about future events is miserable, since it suffers before the misfortune arrives.

Colour a run of selected text. Multiple colours = multiple meanings (e.g. yellow = key idea, blue = disagree). Needs a text layer.

Underline & strikethrough v1

He who fears death will never do anything worthy of a living man. Delete this weak clause.

Same engine as highlight, different look. Underline to emphasise; strikethrough to mark text you reject. Cheap once highlight exists.

Margin note v1

The whole future is uncertain: live immediately, for tomorrow is not owed to you.
cf. Seneca — same idea, letter 101

A written note attached to a spot or a highlight. Shown in the margin (or a pop-up when you tap the anchor). This is your "marginalia" — thinking alongside the text.

Bookmark / dog-ear v1

Chapter IV — On the shortness of life. A page you want to return to with one tap.

Mark a whole page to jump back to later — like folding a corner (dog-ear) or a ribbon. A named list of these = your "jump back to" menu.

Freehand ink later

Draw anywhere with a pen or finger — circle a word, scrawl an arrow, sketch a diagram in the margin.

Free drawing over the page. Works even on scanned books. But it's a whole separate input system (pen pressure, eraser, touch) — I'd defer it.

Region highlight scan fallback

boxed area
For scanned books with no text layer, you can't select words — so you draw a box around part of the image instead.

A rectangle over an image region — the only way to "highlight" a scanned page. Lets scanned books still get marks & notes, minus true text selection.
